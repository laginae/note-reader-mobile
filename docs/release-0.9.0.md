# Cozy Read Aloud Mobile 0.9.0

- New installations use Everyday reading. Reading content also offers Academic detail or the saved Custom preferences. Existing settings, voices and consent do not switch to a new preset on upgrade.
- Simple tables introduce headers once; numeric comparisons keep their labels. Simple functions follow the language of the passage; natural intervals preserve their endpoints. Smart omissions remain in effect.
- The optional OCR settings link to the matching iOS/Android resource pack and installation guide. OCR remains experimental, off by default, one page at a time and requires a separate verified resource pack. Background playback remains experimental; no new physical-device validation is claimed.
